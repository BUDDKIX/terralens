07 / Method and limitations

RGB absolute difference after a 3 × 3 mean filter, threshold segmentation, four-connected regions, and minimum-area filtering. Transparent and near-black no-data pixels are excluded. No automatic classification, cloud mask, spectral index, or image registration is applied.

Identical requested EPSG:4326 bounds and dimensions. No independent registration verification.

  • Public browse imagery; seasonal and atmospheric variations can affect visual differences.
  • This is not labeled ground truth for accuracy measurement.
  • Shadows, lighting, seasonal differences, and residual misalignment can create false positives.
  • Processing downsampling and thresholds can omit small or subtle changes.
  • Geographic area and operational route safety are not inferred.
